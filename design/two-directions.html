A and C, now clickable.

I removed the big "new roles" number. In its place is a thin line that says how many roles match, which filters are on (click a filter's × to remove it) and when the list was last updated.

Everything on both screens works, so try it like a real site:

  • Type in search, change Fit, switch filters on and off, change the sort
  • Save, Mark applied and Hide (with Undo), then check the Saved and Applied tabs
  • On the phones, tap a job to open the full job page, then tap Back
  • On a laptop, point at a screen and use ↑ ↓ (or J K) to move, S to save, / to search

The laptop and phone in each direction share the same data, so a job saved on one shows as saved on the other.
