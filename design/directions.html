Three directions. Pick one.

Both articles make the same point. AI-made sites look alike because they stay in the safe middle: soft rounded cards, one pleasant colour, the same fonts. The fix is to commit to one named style, with exact fonts and colours, and to compare a few very different options before building.

So instead of a third guess, here are three directions that are deliberately unlike each other and unlike the last two previews. Each shows the same nine real jobs on a laptop and a phone. Click a job to open it. In C, click a colour tile to filter.

You can also mix, for example A's layout with C's colours.

Direction ATimetable

Jobs read like a station departures board. Everything sits on a strict grid, so you can scan fifty roles in a minute.

Inspired by
Swiss railway timetables and 1960s Swiss posters
Fonts
Archivo, squeezed narrow for headlines and numbers. JetBrains Mono for pay, dates and facts.
Colours
#E6EAEE paper#0A1A2F ink#D92D20 signal red
The bold bit
Giant narrow numbers for new jobs and fit. The job you're reading flips to dark.
The risk
Can feel cold and strict. Square corners everywhere.

Open this page on a laptop to see the laptop layout too.

Direction BTerminal

A trading terminal for your job search: dark, dense and built for the keyboard.

Inspired by
The market terminals people use in banking and finance
Fonts
IBM Plex Sans Condensed for text, IBM Plex Mono for data
Colours
#0B0F17 night#E7EAF0 text#FFB020 amber#8DB0FF ISB blue
The bold bit
A ticker of new jobs by function across the top, and a key for every action (J and K to move, S to save, Enter to apply).
The risk
Only works dark. Heavy for people who don't love dense data.

Open this page on a laptop to see the laptop layout too.

Direction CCampus poster

Each function has its own colour and shape, so you can spot your lane at a glance.

Inspired by
Bauhaus posters: primary colours, circles, squares and triangles
Fonts
Jost (geometric, Futura-like) for headings, Work Sans for text
Colours
#F1F2F4 page#141414 ink#D7263D red#F5C400 yellow#1D4ED8 blue
The bold bit
Function tiles sized by how many jobs each has. They double as the filter.
The risk
More playful. The colours have to mean the same thing everywhere or it turns noisy.
